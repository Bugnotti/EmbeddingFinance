Your Journey in 6 Stages

1

Ideation & Setup

Share your startup idea, selected industry, and operating country. The app creates your personalized roadmap instantly.

2

Learning & Building

Complete short lessons and actionable tasks. Build real startup artifacts as you learn. Progress tracks automatically.

3
